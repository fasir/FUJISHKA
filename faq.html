It provides real-time centralized control over Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Accounting, HR & Payroll, CRM, Production, and Business Analytics from one unified dashboard.

Fujeeka is an advanced Android/iOS mobile Van Sales app built for field distribution salesmen. It allows van drivers to issue spot invoices, record cash/credit sales, manage van stock balances, track customer payment collections, and view daily sales targets. If internet connection is lost in remote areas, Fujeeka operates 100% offline and automatically syncs all transaction data with the main ERP once connectivity is restored.

Yes! Fujishka R-POS connects seamlessly via Bluetooth to handheld thermal printers and mobile receipt printers. Salesmen can instantly print thermal bills, tax invoices, receipt vouchers, and stock load sheets right at the customer's doorstep.

Fleet managers can assign optimized daily visit routes to each van driver, track real-time GPS locations of sales vans on live maps, verify customer visit check-ins via geo-fencing, and review end-of-day sales reconciliation vs stock loaded.

Fujishka Hybrid POS combines high-speed desktop billing with offline database resilience. If your retail outlet or supermarket loses internet during peak billing hours, cashiers can continue barcode scanning and cash/card checkout without interruption.

Yes! Fujishka ERP is 100% compliant with Saudi Arabia's ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) Phase 2 integration requirements. The system automatically generates Cryptographic Stamp UUIDs, Phase 2 compliant QR Codes, XML electronic invoices, and submits them directly to the ZATCA portal API in real time.
