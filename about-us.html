Who We Are

Trusted Technology Partner for Businesses Worldwide

We are Fujishka Solutions — a technology-first company with a singular aim: to develop, maintain and optimize profitable software solutions for our clients, exceeding their expectations in product quality, value, support and customer service.

Over the years, we have carved our identity as a trustworthy web and mobile app developer by expanding our scope while retaining quality. We have developed and integrated our Fujishka Inventory & Cloud ERP Systems for well-established domestic and international organizations.

  • Our portfolio spans small start-ups to well-known brands across Asia and the Middle East.
  • We have developed and integrated our Fujishka Inventory System for well-established domestic and international organizations.
  • Based in the South Indian city of Calicut (Kozhikode), established since 2010 and continuously growing.
  • Serving clients across India, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and GCC with localized, ZATCA-compliant solutions.

What We Do

We Do Things We Love to Do

Our team is always passionate about developing and executing products that meet the requirements of our valuable clients who seek a technological transition in their conventional business models.

Cloud ERP Software

Comprehensive cloud-based ERP system (FUJISKA-ERP) that manages sales, purchase, inventory, accounts and reporting from a unified platform accessible from anywhere.

Van Sales App (Fujeeka)

Mobile Van Sales application that enables field salesmen to take orders, manage van inventory, collect payments and plan routes — all from their smartphones, online or offline.

Inventory Management

Advanced inventory system integrated with our ERP for real-time stock tracking, warehouse management and multi-location control tailored for distributors and traders.

Web Design & Development

Professional website design and development for businesses of all sizes, from small start-ups to well-known brands, spanning Asia and the Middle East.

App Development

Custom mobile application development for iOS and Android platforms, designed to meet the specific operational needs of modern businesses seeking digital transformation.

Digital Marketing & Branding

Strategic digital marketing and branding solutions to help businesses grow their online presence, reach their target audience and build a strong, recognizable brand identity.

Our Evolution

The Story of Fujishka

From a small software development team in Calicut to a global cloud ERP & Van Sales provider trusted by 1000+ businesses.

2010

Company Foundation

Fujishka was established in Calicut (Kozhikode), India, with a core mission to provide custom web and business application solutions for local enterprises.

2015

Launch of Fujishka Inventory System

Expanded product scope by introducing dedicated inventory and billing software for wholesale distributors and retail businesses.

2018

GCC Market Expansion

Opened regional operations in Saudi Arabia and UAE to serve growing demand for localized ERP, VAT compliance, and Arabic-first business software.

2022

Fujeeka Van Sales & ZATCA Integration

Launched Fujeeka Van Sales App and integrated full Phase-2 ZATCA e-Invoicing compliance into FUJISKA-ERP for Saudi businesses.

2026 & Beyond

FujiBot AI & Global Leadership

Pioneered FujiBot AI assistant inside FUJISKA-ERP for automated inventory forecasting, smart reporting, and voice/chat queries.
